## Logo Specifications

### File Format
- **Format**: PNG with transparent background
- **Size**: 200x200px recommended
- **Quality**: High resolution for crisp display
- **Style**: Professional, clean design

### Color Guidelines
- Use official brand colors where possible
- Ensure good contrast against dark background
- Maintain professional appearance

## Implementation

1. Add these logo files to your `/public` folder
2. The TrustBadges component will automatically display them
3. Test the display to ensure proper alignment and sizing
